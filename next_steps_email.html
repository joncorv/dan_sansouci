Proposed pages

This is just a starting point. Please tell me if anything feels unnecessary or if I’ve left something out.

Home

  • Large images of your paintings and other work.
  • A simple menu for moving around the site.
  • Your name placed prominently at the top or center of the page. I’ll show you a few options.

Books

  • A collection of the books you’ve illustrated, including books that are out of print.
  • We can give the book covers a subtle shadow so they feel like real objects on the page.

Individual book pages

  • Clicking a book will open a page about that particular book.
  • Each page could include the title, publication date, author and other contributors, publisher, and a short description.
  • We could also include one or two illustrations from inside the book when they’re available.

Paintings

  • A main page showing the different types of paintings, with one featured image for each group.
  • For example, visitors could choose landscapes, figures, or any other groups you’d like to use.

Painting galleries

  • Each group of paintings would have its own gallery.
  • We could add a short introduction and a featured image for each group.
  • Clicking a painting could open it at a larger size. Visitors could then move forward or backward through the gallery like a slideshow.

About Dan

  • We can begin with the biography from your current site.
  • I’d give the page a more polished, magazine-like layout and add one or more photographs of you.

Contact

  • A straightforward way for people to get in touch—and we’ll make sure it works properly this time!
